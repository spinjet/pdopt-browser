## Sampling

Choose a query with current results and generate seeded draws from its joint posterior. Within each selected numerical bin, the sampler draws uniformly from the intersection of the bin and the finite sampling bounds. Brush axes or select points to inspect the same rows across plots and the table.

To predict more variables, select inputs present in the sample or supply fixed values. Choose outputs, fit or reuse surrogates, and review held-out validation before applying the predictions. The ZIP export identifies sampled, fixed and predicted columns. Variation in predicted columns reflects variation across draws and does not quantify the model’s residual uncertainty.
